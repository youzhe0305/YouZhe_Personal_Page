Citations, month by month.論文引用，逐月看清楚。

Google Scholar counts citations by year. CiteCurve shows every month — when a paper took off, how fast it's growing now, and exactly who cited it. Google Scholar 只告訴你每年被引用幾次。CiteCurve 把它拆成每個月：什麼時候開始爆發、現在成長得多快、那個月是誰引用了它。

Add to Chrome加到 Chrome How it works運作方式

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons.即將上架 Chrome 線上應用程式商店與 Edge 附加元件。

Everything the yearly number hides年度數字看不到的，這裡都有

One click turns a citation count into a timeline you can actually read.按一下，就把一個引用數變成看得懂的時間線。

Three charts, one timeline三張圖，一條時間軸

Citations per month with a 3-month average, the running total, and a trend line that shows whether citations are speeding up or slowing down.每月引用數與近 3 個月平均、累積引用，以及看出引用在加速還是減速的斜率圖。

Right where you read在你讀論文的地方

A button on Google Scholar and arXiv. On any other page, open CiteCurve to pick up the paper automatically, or right-click a title.Google Scholar 和 arXiv 上直接有按鈕。在其他網頁打開 CiteCurve 會自動帶入論文，或反白標題按右鍵。

Private by design不蒐集任何資料

No account, no tracking, no server of our own. Only the paper you look up is sent to Semantic Scholar or OpenAlex.不用帳號、沒有追蹤，也沒有我們自己的伺服器。只有你查的論文會送到 Semantic Scholar 或 OpenAlex。

Click a month. See who cited it.點一個月份，看是誰引用了它。

Every bar opens the list of citing papers from that month, with links.每一根長條都能展開那個月引用它的論文清單，附連結。

  • Dates papers by their first arXiv version, so preprints count when they appear有 arXiv 版本的論文用首版月份，預印本一出現就算數
  • Compare with Google Scholar's yearly counts side by side可並列 Google Scholar 的逐年數字對照
  • Export the monthly numbers or every citing paper as CSV月份統計與所有引用論文都能匯出 CSV
  • English and Traditional Chinese, light and dark mode支援英文與繁體中文、淺色與深色模式

How it works運作方式

Pick a paper選一篇論文

Click the button on Scholar or arXiv, right-click a title, or type a title, arXiv ID or DOI.在 Scholar 或 arXiv 按按鈕、反白標題按右鍵，或直接輸入標題、arXiv ID、DOI。

We fetch who cited it抓取所有引用它的論文

Straight from Semantic Scholar or OpenAlex, open scholarly databases. Results are kept on your device for a day.直接從開放的學術資料庫 Semantic Scholar 或 OpenAlex 取得，結果只在你的電腦上保留一天。

Read the curve看懂曲線

Each citing paper lands in the month it appeared. Hover for numbers, click for papers.每篇引用論文依出現的月份排入。滑過看數字，點一下看論文。

See where a paper is heading.看清一篇論文正往哪裡走。
